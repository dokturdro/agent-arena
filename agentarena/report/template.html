Leaderboard

Bradley-Terry ratings on the Elo scale, fit from pairwise battles on identical task instances. Ranks follow the Arena convention: a model only ranks below another whose 95% interval lies entirely above its own.

Reliability: pass^k

Chance that all k attempts at the same task succeed. A steep drop means a flaky agent.

Method

  • Every model runs the same tool-calling scaffold, the same prompts and the same budgets.
  • Tool-use tasks are graded on the final world state, compared with the state produced by replaying gold actions, plus required emails and no stray side effects.
  • Research tasks use a synthetic corpus of fictional companies, so answers cannot come from memorized knowledge. Success requires a correct answer and at least one verbatim quote from a gold document.
  • Confidence intervals are 95%, from a clustered bootstrap over task instances (pass@1) or over battles (ratings).
